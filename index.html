⚡ CGVyapam DEO/Grade-03 — Exam Strategy

Paper 140 marks ka hai: GS 50 + Computer 30 + Reasoning 30 + Maths 30. Sabse zyada marks GS mein hain — ise seriously lo.
CG Special GK timetable mein fully cover hai — janjatiya, sanskriti, khaniij, van, muhavare sab include hain.
Samanya Vigyan aur Paryavaran naya addition hai — yahan se 4–6 questions aate hain, ignore mat karna.
Computer mein Antivirus, OS, Multimedia, Printer types — ye sab official syllabus mein hain, MCQ pakka aate hain.
Maths mein Speed-Time-Distance aur Simple Interest — ye topics pehle timetable mein nahi the, ab fully included hain.
English Typing 30 WPM + Hindi Typing 25 WPM — skill test qualify karna mandatory hai, daily practice karo.
Upar chips click karte jao jab topic complete ho — syllabus coverage % automatically update hoga dashboard mein.
Sunday dopehar mein pakka aram karo — brain ko reset time chahiye. Overload se performance girti hai.
